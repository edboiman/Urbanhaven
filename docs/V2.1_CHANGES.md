# Urban Haven 2.1 changes

**Current package: 2.1.1.** The features below were introduced in 2.1.0. The subsequent source audit and corrections are documented in SOURCE_AUDIT.md; storage references are in SHELF_LIFE_REFERENCE.md. Current verification is in TEST_RESULTS.md.

## Map paths and local context

- Multiple named offline waypoint paths, saved automatically, with up to 100 ordered stops.
- Add saved markers or map center; reorder, reverse, mark visited, add notes and export GPX.
- Dashed path lines and numbered stops on the canvas; straight-line distances and initial true bearings. No road routing, travel-time calculation, magnetic correction, safe-crossing validation or live conditions.
- Farm / garden marker category and optional local JPEG, PNG or WebP pictures. Input limited to 10 MB; pictures are resized to a maximum 960 × 720 and converted to local JPEG, with a bounded data URL in the marker record. No photo uploads or bundled farm photo database.
- Six sourced USGS river coordinates with accuracy and historical-status notes. Geographic references do not identify safe access, drinking water or legal crossings. Permission is needed for farms and private land.
- The existing generalized Michigan map and regional packs are retained. They do not become street-level maps merely because the path is precise.

## Growing food

The default crop page uses growing-space, goal and crop dropdowns. Suburban beds, urban containers, supported indoor growing and indoor outages receive different advice. Detailed crop profiles and the frost calendar remain available without crowding the default view.

Seed packet groups show nutrition purpose, season limits, variety selection, storage and germination tests. Shelf-life examples assume ideal storage and are not viability guarantees. Seed potatoes are a separate seasonal purchase. Indoor greens cannot replace stored calories or an immediate food supply.

## Pantry and cooking

Nine core food groups come before six useful extras. Each has common examples, approximate quality shelf lives, opened-package rules, storage and water / fuel needs. Product-specific dates and safety conditions still apply. Existing inventory-based meals and the 14-day planner remain accessible.

A dedicated Outdoor cooking page has ten original pantry recipes, selectable by recipe or vegetarian / one-pot filter, with ingredients, equipment, steps, water, heat and leftovers. Outdoor-only combustion, fire restrictions and no-cook alternatives are prominent. Recipes have not been kitchen tested in this session.

## Health, radio and pets

- Ten additional guides address common concerns during disrupted care. Symptoms requiring help, initial actions, conservative care and things to avoid are shown by concern. This is educational content, not independent clinical review or a substitute for emergency care.
- A dedicated radio page has 28 Michigan NOAA transmitters and 20 selected AM/FM stations, saved presets and a user-entered last-heard date. Hand-crank power does not imply a weather-band receiver. No station uptime, reception or disaster broadcasting is guaranteed; no live audio or live status is provided.
- A dedicated pet page has a 12-item checkable kit, eight care / evacuation topics and multiple offline pet records. Individual daily food and water entries scale to the household planning duration; the app does not invent species-wide doses or rations. Medicines, contacts, identification and evacuation notes can be saved.

## Existing data

Database name and native IndexedDB version 20 remain unchanged. New records use the existing plans store; marker photo fields are optional. Schema 1 and 2 backups remain accepted. Routes, photos, radio logs and pet plans are included in unencrypted exports; the sensitive-notebook option only filters notebook entries. Imported PDF originals and downloaded map packs remain separate.

The app still requires a verified secure-context install before offline use. The build contains no external API dependency for the new features. Source links open online; the actual guidance and frequency lists are bundled.
