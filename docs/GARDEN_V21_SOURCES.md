# Simplified growing and seed-reserve guidance

Reviewed 2026-10-31 America/Detroit. The 2.1.1 audit adds corrections and current seed-recall limits in SOURCE_AUDIT.md. Existing Michigan crop and frost sources remain in GARDEN_SOURCES.md and the crop records.

- MSU Extension, Container gardening with vegetables & herbs: https://migarden.msu.edu/uploads/files/containerGarden-VeggiesHerbs.pdf — sunny sites, compact varieties, potting mix, drainage and container water needs. The canr.msu.edu page returned 403 in this session; the official PDF was available.
- UMN Extension, Starting seeds indoors: https://extension.umn.edu/planting-and-growing-guides/starting-seeds-indoors — stable warmth, clean seed-starting media, drainage and limitations of windowsills. Lamp distances are fixture-specific.
- UMN Extension, Saving vegetable seeds: https://extension.umn.edu/planting-and-growing-guides/saving-vegetable-seeds — cool, dry sealed storage; open-pollinated versus hybrid seed; packet labeling and reputable suppliers.
- Iowa State Extension, How to store seeds and test germination rates: https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/how-store-seeds-and-test-germination-rates — approximate ideal-storage examples for individual crops and the need for an actual germination test. Other extension tables differ; these are planning estimates, not guarantees.

The four environment choices and packet groupings are app editorial selections. They do not imply tested yield, site suitability or emergency self-sufficiency. Household potable water and immediate food take priority. Existing planting details, frost dates and harvest origins remain available in expandable profiles and the Season plan.
