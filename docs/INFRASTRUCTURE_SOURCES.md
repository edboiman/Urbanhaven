# Infrastructure content source review

Source checks performed: 2026-10-31. This is a source-backed educational draft, not a clinical, engineering, fire-service or legal review. Physical situations and current official instructions override general reference text.

## Scope and limitations

- 26 complete guides: power (6), climate (5), communications (5), shelter (5), repairs (5).
- The battery calculator explanation uses dimensional arithmetic; it does not assert particular battery efficiency, surge capacity or medical-device suitability. Solar yield is deliberately not quantified.
- Generator safety uses CDC/FEMA outdoor clearance and no-backfeeding guidance. No DIY transfer-switch, gas restoration, exposed electrical work or structural repair instructions are provided.
- Heat stroke, suspected hypothermia, fire, gas leaks and CO alarms escalate to emergency help; no medication doses are given.
- FCC licensing was checked against official published 2024 Part 95 and the 2017 FCC rulemaking. The FCC service web pages returned access errors during verification. Check current FCC rules before radio purchase or transmission; no license fees or frequency list are embedded.
- General communication advice does not promise network availability, text-to-911, responder radio monitoring or device hardware control. Offline maps are a separately downloaded tool, not an implemented app feature.
- American Red Cross frozen-pipe material is paraphrased; no document is redistributed. The remaining citations are authoritative government publications. Logos and third-party assets are not reused.
- Michigan shelter guidance is qualified by incident-specific instructions; ventilation sealing is not a universal recommendation.
