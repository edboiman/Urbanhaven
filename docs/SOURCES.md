**What is official vs. assumed.** Each checklist item is labeled in the app:

* *Official recommendation* — the item (and, for water, the per-person amount) appears in the cited source.
* *App assumption* — this app's planning default. These are: the **14-day duration** (Ready.gov says “several days”; CDC says try for 2 weeks of water), **2,000 kcal/person/day**, one flashlight / whistle / power bank / utensil set / bedding per person, two spare battery sets and two dust masks per person, one heavy-duty bag per person per day, toilet paper 2 rolls/person/week, absorbent 1 bag/household/week, 2 refill containers, maintenance intervals (lights and power banks every 90 days, OTC medicines every 180 days, plans yearly).
* *Your amount* — no defensible universal number: extra hygiene water, pet water and pet food, cash, infant formula and diapers, prescriptions, flushing water. These stay “Needed — set an amount” until the household enters one.

No item claims FEMA or any agency certifies a “14-day kit”.
