# Urban Haven 2.2 — phone UX and food update

Reviewed and prepared 2026-11-01 America/Detroit.

## Design review and implementation

The prior checklist required inventory entry before most supply items could be completed. Dense explanation, five status summaries and multiple filters competed with the action. Food pages mixed shopping priorities, a planner and two recipe formats. On a narrow screen, navigation wrapped into several rows and pushed the useful list below the fold.

This release keeps the warm paper/forest palette and type system. It changes the hierarchy and interactions:

- Every applicable checklist item has a large checkbox and a label that can be tapped directly. Details is a separate action for quantities, sources, notes and customization. Checked items stay visible by default, with an optional “Only show what is left” filter.
- A manual check means “prepared according to this household.” It does not create supplies or add liters/calories. Actual stock, shopping gaps and safety-date exclusions still use inventory. Explicit unchecking overrides automatic inventory readiness. Confirmations remain until changed; maintenance reminders remain available in the tools disclosure.
- Pantry essentials and useful extras use searchable expandable rows, with consistent unopened-quality, opened-food, water/fuel and storage labels. Planning/safety tools remain available below the main list.
- A unified library contains 47 recipes: 24 from the uploaded cooking pack, ten previous stove recipes and 13 previous pantry recipes. All have ingredient gathering, separate numbered cooking steps, water/heat facts, equipment and safety notes. The original saved meal-plan recipe IDs still resolve.
- Recipe filters distinguish heat requirements and refrigeration. Chilled overnight oats are excluded unless safe refrigeration is explicitly available; conditional instant oats are conservatively treated as needing heat. Canned/cooked beans are never silently replaced with dry beans.
- Nutrition uses the household's editable calorie estimate and gives food-group priorities. 2,000 kcal is identified as a general label reference, not a universal minimum. No precise recipe nutrient totals are invented.
- Twenty-one portion/preparation-specific oxalate references are bundled and linked from relevant ingredient lists. Missing ingredients are unknown, not zero. No complete-recipe oxalate total or clinical daily oxalate target is generated.

Native database name/version and backup schema remain unchanged. Optional prepared/preparedOn fields are validated and included in exports; older backups remain readable. A pre-2.2 app cannot preserve fields it does not know if it re-exports a new backup. Back up before changing versions.

## Content provenance

The user's uploaded “urban_haven_emergency_recipes(2).md” supplied the 24 original practical recipes and seven cooking-basics sections. Quantities, water, ingredient state and steps were kept together. Conditional oat preparation was strengthened to require appropriate product instructions and actual cold storage. Preparation times are estimates; no kitchen, fuel-consumption or nutrition-laboratory testing was performed.

Primary technique/safety references consulted:

- USA Rice, cooking methods: https://www.usarice.com/thinkrice/how-to/how-to-cook-rice
- Iowa State, lentils: https://spendsmart.extension.iastate.edu/pantry-picks/lentils/
